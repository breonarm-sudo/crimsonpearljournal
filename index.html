About the Journal

Mission Statement

The Crimson Pearl Journal exists to give OU students of every major space to engage in writing and journalistic pursuits. We cover current, impactful decisions, people, and moments shaping our university and the Norman–Moore–OKC community, and we believe journalism is a skill worth building alongside any career path.
